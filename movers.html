About gainers, losers and the Fear & Greed Index

Gainers are coins whose price rose the most in the last 24 hours, and losers are the ones that fell the most. Big moves can reverse quickly, so check a coin's chart before you act. The Fear & Greed Index summarizes the market mood from 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed), and it is a rough guide, not a signal to buy or sell.
