How to read this

Buy is the Naira you pay to get 1 USDT. Sell is the Naira you receive when you sell 1 USDT. Best is the single best advert at the moment. Typical is the middle price of the top five adverts, which is closer to what you will really get. The gap compares the typical buy price with the official dollar rate. P2P prices are usually higher than the official rate because of demand for dollars.

Be careful. These are adverts posted by individual traders and prices change fast. Always trade inside the exchange's escrow, never pay outside the platform, and confirm the final price and limits before you pay. Very small or very large adverts are left out, so the price you get may differ.
