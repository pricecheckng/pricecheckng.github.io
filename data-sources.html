Data sources

PriceCheck NG shows information from public data providers. This page lists them. All data is for information only and is not financial advice.

Live prices

Prices are collected from the public market data of many exchanges, including Binance, Bybit, OKX, KuCoin, Gate.io, MEXC, Bitget, HTX, Crypto.com, LBank, WhiteBIT, Poloniex, DigiFinex and XT. For each coin we use a middle price across exchanges and ignore prices that look wrong. For coins that are not listed on these exchanges, we use data from decentralised exchanges and the other sources below.

Exchange rates

Naira and other currencies are converted from the US dollar price at market exchange rates. Rates By Exchange Rate API.

Market statistics

  • CoinGecko: the coin list, logos, circulating supply and some price data.
  • Live Coin Watch: price, volume and supply for some coins, such as stablecoins.
  • DexScreener: price and trading volume for tokens traded on decentralised exchanges.

Market cap is calculated by PriceCheck NG as our live price times the circulating supply, and rank follows market cap. 24-hour volume is the trading volume on the exchanges we read, so it can be lower than totals shown by sites that count more exchanges. Tokenized assets, such as funds, bonds and stocks, are labelled and kept out of the main ranking.

Tokens that are not in our list

The Track any token page uses public data from DexScreener and GeckoTerminal. This shows one trading pool, so the price can differ from other sources. Contract checks for honeypots, trading taxes and owner powers come from GoPlus Security. These checks show warning signs only and are not a safety guarantee.

News

Headlines come from publishers' public feeds. Each story opens on the publisher's own site.

Data can be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Check important numbers on more than one source. If you see a mistake, write to support@pricecheckng.com.ng.
