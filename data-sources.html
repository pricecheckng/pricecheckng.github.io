PriceCheck NG

Data sources

PriceCheck NG shows information from public data providers. This page lists them. All data is for information only and is not financial advice.

Live prices

Prices are collected from public market data of several exchanges, including Binance, Bybit, OKX, KuCoin, Gate.io, MEXC, Bitget and HTX. Where an exchange does not list a coin, we use other public market data sources.

Exchange rates

Naira and other currencies are converted from the US dollar price at market exchange rates. Rates By Exchange Rate API.

Market statistics

  • CoinPaprika: market data for some coins.
  • CoinLore: market data and circulating supply.
  • Live Coin Watch: circulating supply for some coins.
  • CoinGecko: some older coin data and logos, while we move to other sources.

Market cap and rank are calculated by PriceCheck NG from our live price and the circulating supply.

Tokens that are not in our list

The Track any token page uses public data from DexScreener and GeckoTerminal. This shows one trading pool, so the price can differ from other sources.

News

Headlines come from publishers' public feeds. Each story opens on the publisher's own site.

Data can be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Check important numbers on more than one source. If you see a mistake, write to support@pricecheckng.com.ng.
