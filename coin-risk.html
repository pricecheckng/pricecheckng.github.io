Search a coin and see warning signs from its market data. This is not a safety guarantee.

How the label works

  • Market cap: very small coins are easier to move and manipulate. What is market cap?
  • Trading volume: if volume is very low compared with market cap, it can be hard to sell at a fair price.
  • 24h price move: a big swing in one day means the price is unstable right now.

The label only uses these numbers. It cannot see who is behind a coin, whether the project is honest, or whether it is a scam. Read how to avoid crypto scams before buying anything new.
