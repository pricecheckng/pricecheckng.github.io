What is the Fear and Greed Index?

A free beginner lesson on the 0 to 100 mood score of the crypto market.

1. The idea

The Fear and Greed Index gives one number from 0 to 100 that shows how people feel about the crypto market right now. Low numbers mean fear and high numbers mean greed. It is a mood gauge.

2. Try the gauge

The labels above are a simple guide. Different sources draw the lines a little differently.

3. What goes into it

The score is built from several things, such as how much the price is moving, how much is being traded, what people say on social media and how strong Bitcoin is compared with the rest of the market. Each part is mixed into one number.

4. The famous idea

A well-known saying is to be careful when others are greedy and careful when others are fearful. Some people think fear can mean prices are low, and greed can mean prices are too high. But this is only an idea. Markets can stay fearful or greedy for a long time, so the index is not a buy or sell signal.

5. How to use it well

  • Use it to check your own feelings. If you feel an urge to buy because everyone is excited, the index may be high too.
  • Combine it with other things: the price chart, the news and your own plan.
  • Never buy or sell only because of one number.
