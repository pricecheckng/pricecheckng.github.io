Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about PriceCheck NG. Last reviewed 7 November 2026.

What is PriceCheck NG?

PriceCheck NG is a crypto price tracker built for Nigeria. It shows live prices for hundreds of coins in naira and other currencies, with market cap, volume, charts, news and a daily and weekly market summary.

Is PriceCheck NG an exchange? Can I buy crypto here?

No. PriceCheck NG does not hold your money and you cannot buy or sell here. It only shows information. To trade, you need to use an exchange or a P2P market of your choice.

Where do your prices come from?

From public market data of more than 20 exchanges. We use a middle price and ignore prices that look wrong. The full explanation is on the Methodology page and the list of providers is on Data sources.

Why is the naira price different from what I see on my exchange or P2P app?

We convert the dollar price to naira at a market exchange rate. Local exchanges and P2P markets use their own naira rates, which can be higher or lower. Treat our naira price as a guide and check the rate on the platform before you trade.

Why is a price here different from CoinMarketCap or CoinGecko?

Different sites read different exchanges and use different methods, so small differences are normal. Market cap can also differ when sites use different supply numbers.

How often do prices update?

Prices refresh every few minutes. A coin page shows when its price was last updated.

How is market cap worked out?

Market cap is our live price multiplied by the circulating supply. Rank follows market cap. See Market cap in the glossary.

Why does a coin not have a page?

Coin pages are made for up to 500 coins that rank highly and have recent prices and real trading volume. A coin that stops meeting these conditions loses its page until it qualifies again. You can still look for coins with the search box on the home page.

Who writes the daily and weekly summaries?

An AI model writes them from our own market numbers and is told not to give reasons, predictions or advice. Every summary says it was written by AI. You can read them on the daily market summary page, and the weekly recap is posted on our Telegram channel @pricecheckNG.

Is PriceCheck NG financial advice?

No. Everything here is information only. Crypto prices can fall quickly and you can lose money. Never invest money you cannot afford to lose, and read our guide to avoiding scams.

I found a wrong price or a mistake. What should I do?

Please write to support@pricecheckng.com.ng with the coin name and the page. We will check it.

Where can I learn what the crypto words mean?

Read the glossary. It explains terms like market cap, stablecoin, ATH and dominance in plain English.
