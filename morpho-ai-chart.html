What is the Morpho AI chart?

It is a live Morpho (MORPHO) candlestick chart with a chat. You ask in plain words and PCN AI reads the candles, draws support and resistance, adds indicators and explains each mark.

Which exchanges can I see MORPHO on?

You can switch the chart between exchanges such as Binance, OKX, Bybit, Bitget, MEXC and Gate, and overlay a second exchange to compare prices in percent.

Is this financial advice?

No. The levels come from past price swings and are not predictions. PriceCheck NG is a price tracker and does not let you trade.
