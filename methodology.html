How PriceCheck NG works out its numbers

Last reviewed 7 November 2026. This page explains our method in plain words. For the list of providers, see Data sources.

Live prices

We read public market data from more than 20 exchanges, including Binance, Bybit, OKX, KuCoin, Gate.io, MEXC, Bitget, Coinbase, Kraken and others. For each coin we use a middle price across those exchanges and ignore prices that look wrong, such as a single exchange far away from the rest. For coins that are not listed on those exchanges, we use data from decentralised exchanges (through DexScreener). Prices refresh every few minutes, and a coin page shows when its price was last updated.

Market cap and rank

Market cap is calculated by PriceCheck NG as our live price multiplied by the circulating supply. Rank follows market cap. Supply figures come from CoinGecko and Live Coin Watch. Because supply data is reported by third parties, it can be late or wrong for some coins, and so can the market cap that depends on it.

24-hour volume

24-hour volume is the value traded on the exchanges we read. Sites that count more exchanges can show a larger number, so our volume can be lower than the totals you see elsewhere.

Naira and other currencies

Every price starts in US dollars and is converted into naira and other currencies at market exchange rates from Exchange Rate API. The naira price on a coin page is therefore the dollar price converted at that rate. It is not the price you would pay on a local exchange or a P2P market, which can be different.

USDT to naira P2P rate

The P2P page shows the rate people are offering for USDT in naira on the peer-to-peer markets of Bybit, OKX, Bitget and MEXC. These offers change quickly and depend on the payment method, the amount and the seller, so treat the number as a guide and check the offer before you trade.

Fear & Greed Index

The 0 to 100 score comes from alternative.me. We show it as published. Our own pages explain how to read it in plain words.

Daily and weekly summaries

The daily market summary and the weekly recap are written by an AI model from PriceCheck NG's own market numbers (total market cap, volume, Bitcoin dominance, Fear & Greed, top gainers and losers). The model is told to use only that data and not to give reasons, predictions or advice. Every summary says it was written by AI. Always check the live numbers.

News

Headlines come from the public feeds of publishers. Each story opens on the publisher's own site, and we do not edit the stories.

Categories and coin facts

Categories (such as memes, AI or gaming), all-time high, coin age, number of exchanges, maximum supply and official links come from third-party data providers. A coin can belong to more than one category, and the facts on a coin page can be a little behind the live price.

Tokenized assets

Tokens that represent real-world assets, such as funds, bonds or stocks, are labelled as tokenized and kept out of the main ranking, because they behave differently from ordinary coins.

Which coins get a page

Coin pages are made for up to 500 coins that rank highly by market cap and have recent prices and real trading volume. If a coin stops meeting those conditions, its page is removed, and it comes back when the coin qualifies again.

Delays, mistakes and corrections

Crypto data can be delayed, incomplete or wrong. A price can look different from another site because we use different exchanges and a different method. Check important numbers on more than one source before you act. If you find a mistake, write to support@pricecheckng.com.ng and tell us the coin and the page.

Not financial advice

PriceCheck NG shows information only. It does not hold your money, it is not an exchange, and nothing on the site is a recommendation to buy or sell. Crypto prices can fall quickly, and you can lose money. Read our guide to avoiding scams and our glossary if some words are new to you.
