How to read this page

A fee is what you pay the network to include your transfer. It is not a percentage of what you send. A transfer of $10 and a transfer of $10,000 cost about the same. The fee depends on how busy the network is, not on the amount.

sat/vB is the price per unit of data. A "sat" is the smallest piece of a Bitcoin (one hundred-millionth). A normal transfer is about 140 units, so the cost shown is that number times the fee.

Fast, Medium and Slow mean how soon your transfer is likely to be confirmed: in the next block (about 10 minutes), within about half an hour, or within about an hour.

Mempool is the waiting room. Transfers wait there until a miner puts them in a block. The longer the queue, the higher the fee you need to jump ahead.

A block is a batch of confirmed transfers, found about every 10 minutes. Difficulty adjusts about every two weeks so blocks keep coming at that pace. The halving cuts the reward for miners in half about every four years.

Network data: mempool.space, collected every two minutes. Prices: PriceCheck NG. Figures are estimates and change every minute. This is information only and not financial advice.
