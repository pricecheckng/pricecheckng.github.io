How to get price alerts in Telegram

  1. Open PriceCheck NG and set an alert on a coin (the 🔔 button).
  2. Go to Account and tap the Telegram connect button. It opens the bot and links your account.
  3. When the price hits your target, the bot sends you a message.

Add the bot to your Telegram group

  1. Open the group, tap its name, then Add member.
  2. Search @pricecheck_ng_alerts_bot and add it.
  3. Type a coin name such as how much is ETH. The bot replies in the group.

Frequently asked questions

Is the PriceCheck NG Telegram bot free?

Yes. The bot, the price lookups and the price alerts are free to use.

Can I add the bot to my Telegram crypto group?

Yes. Add @pricecheck_ng_alerts_bot to your group. When a member types something like "how much is BTC" or /price btc, the bot replies with the live price.

How accurate are the price updates?

For major coins the bot reads the price straight from a live exchange feed (Binance, Bybit, OKX and others) at the moment you ask, and the card says which exchange it used. If no live feed is available it shows the price we saved a few minutes ago and says so.

How do I set a price alert?

Set the alert on pricecheckng.com.ng, then connect Telegram from your Account page. Your alerts arrive in the bot as a message.

Does the bot show Naira prices?

Coin prices are shown in US dollars. For Naira, send /usdt to see USDT/Naira P2P buy and sell rates from several exchanges.
