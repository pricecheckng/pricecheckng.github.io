PriceCheck NG

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

PriceCheck NG ("we", "us") shows live crypto prices in US dollars and Nigerian Naira and lets you set price alerts. This page explains what personal information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password, which is stored in scrambled (hashed) form by our sign-in provider. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and basic profile details (such as your name) from Google. We never see your Google password.
  • Alerts you create: the coin, target price, currency and direction, and whether the alert is active, triggered or cancelled.
  • Notification details: if you turn on notifications, your browser gives us a push subscription address so we can send alerts to your device.
  • Technical data: basic logs (such as IP address and browser type) kept by our hosting and database providers for security and reliability.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To check prices against your alerts and send you notifications.
  • To send account emails, such as email confirmation and password reset.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads.

Who handles it for us

  • Supabase stores our database and handles sign-in.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Google provides the optional "Continue with Google" sign-in and the email service used to send account emails.
  • Browser push services run by Google, Apple or Mozilla deliver notifications to your device.
  • CoinGecko supplies price data. We do not send them any of your personal information.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and alerts until you ask us to delete them. You can cancel or delete any alert in the app at any time. To delete your account and all related data, email us at the address below and we will do it within 30 days.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including access rules in our database so that each person can see only their own alerts. No online service can be perfectly secure, so please choose a strong, unique password.

Children

PriceCheck NG is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions or requests: pricecheckng@gmail.com
