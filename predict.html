How it works

A new round opens every day at 7:00 AM Nigerian time. You see Bitcoin's price at the start, and you pick whether it will be higher or lower when the round ends the next morning. You can pick for the first few hours of each round, and your pick is locked once you make it. A correct pick earns 10 points. If the price barely moves (under 0.05%), the round is a draw and nobody gains or loses points.

This is a free game for fun. There is no money, no entry fee and no cash prize, and it is not financial advice.
