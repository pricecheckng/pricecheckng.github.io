How to read the heatmap

Each tile is one coin. The bigger the tile, the bigger the coin's market cap. Green means the price went up over the period you chose, and red means it went down. The stronger the colour, the bigger the move.

It helps you see the mood of the whole market in a few seconds. If most of the map is green, most coins are rising. Stablecoins and wrapped coins are hidden so they don't crowd the picture.
