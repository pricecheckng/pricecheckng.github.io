How to read this heatmap

Each tile is one coin. Green means the price went up in the chosen time, red means it went down, and grey means almost no change. The deeper the colour, the bigger the move. Larger tiles are coins with a bigger market cap.

A mostly green map means the market is rising together. A mostly red map means most coins are falling. Use it to see the mood fast, then open a coin to see its chart.
