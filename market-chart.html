Total crypto market cap, Bitcoin dominance and Fear & Greed over time. Updated every hour.

What these charts show

Total market cap is the combined value of all cryptocurrencies. When it rises, money is flowing into crypto. When it falls, money is flowing out.

Bitcoin dominance is Bitcoin's share of that total. When dominance rises, Bitcoin is doing better than other coins. When it falls, other coins (altcoins) are catching up.

Fear & Greed is a mood score from 0 (extreme fear) to 100 (extreme greed).
