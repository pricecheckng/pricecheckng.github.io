What is a crypto treasury?

A treasury is the money and assets a company keeps in reserve. A crypto treasury company has put part of that reserve into coins such as Bitcoin or Ethereum, usually because it expects the coins to grow in value over time.

The value now is the number of coins multiplied by today's price. Up or down compares that with what the company paid for the coins on average. A company can be up even if the price falls, if it bought early and cheaply.

These companies are mostly listed on stock markets, so their holdings are published in public reports. Counts can change when a company buys, sells or reports late.

Who holds what: public company filings, collected by CoinGecko. Values use PriceCheck NG's live prices. Companies report their holdings every few months or when they announce a purchase, so these numbers can be behind the real amounts. This is information only and not financial advice.
