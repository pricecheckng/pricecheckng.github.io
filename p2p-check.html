How to read the answer

  • Fair: within about 1% to 2% of the market. Small gaps are normal, because agents need to earn something.
  • Expensive or low: you are losing more than 2%. Ask for a better rate or compare with another agent.
  • Too good to be true: a rate far better than the market is often a scam. Read how to avoid crypto scams.
