About PriceCheck NG

PriceCheck NG is a free crypto price tracker. It shows live prices from many exchanges in US dollars, naira and other currencies, so people can compare before they buy, sell or send.

What we do

  • Show live prices and exchange-by-exchange comparisons, with the highest and lowest price and the gap between them.
  • Let you compare coins side by side, set price alerts and get them in Telegram.
  • Offer free tools for Nigerians, such as a naira converter, a USDT P2P rate check and scam-safety lessons.
  • Provide a free price API for developers.

Where the data comes from

Prices are collected directly from public exchange feeds and other published sources, and refreshed every few minutes. See the data sources page for details. Prices are for information only and are not financial advice.
