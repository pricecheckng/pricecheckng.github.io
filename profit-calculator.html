How to use the profit calculator

Choose a coin and your currency, then enter how much you invested and the price per coin when you bought. The sell price starts at today's live price, so you can see your profit or loss right now, or type a different sell price to see what happens if the coin reaches it. If you pay trading fees, enter the percent and it will be taken off on both the buy and the sell.
