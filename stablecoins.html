How to read this page

A stablecoin is a coin built to stay at one dollar. Many Nigerians use USDT to save and to trade, so it matters that the price holds.

Holding $1 means the price is within half a cent (0.5%) of one dollar. Slipping means it is a little off. Off peg means it has moved far from one dollar, which is a warning sign. Do not buy or hold large amounts of a coin that is off peg until the reason is clear.

Supply is how many of the coin exist. If the supply is growing, more people are using it. A fast fall in supply over a week can mean people are cashing out.

No price data means the data source has no market price for that coin, so we cannot say if it holds $1. Small coins (under about $100 million) trade thinly, so their price can jump around and show a warning that fades quickly. A change in supply of hundreds of percent is hidden because it usually comes from a tiny starting amount.
