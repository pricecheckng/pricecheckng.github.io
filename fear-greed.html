What the number means

0–24 Extreme Fear: people are very scared and selling.
25–44 Fear: the market is nervous.
45–55 Neutral: no strong mood either way.
56–74 Greed: people are buying with confidence.
75–100 Extreme Greed: everyone is excited, and prices can be stretched.

How to use it

The index measures the mood of the crypto market. It looks at things like price swings, trading volume, social media activity and how dominant Bitcoin is.

Some traders get careful when everyone is extremely greedy, and look for chances when everyone is extremely fearful. It is a mood gauge, not a signal to buy or sell on its own. Always check the price chart too.
